# Interaction Flow

Version: 1.0

---

# Overview

This document describes the audience journey through PixStars.

---

# Stage 1 — Curiosity

Audience sees:

- Darkness
- Piano notes
- Lamp activation

Question created:

"What is this?"

Emotion:

Curiosity

---

# Stage 2 — Creation

Audience sees:

- Walt drawing
- Human imagination

Question:

"What makes humans special?"

Emotion:

Wonder

---

# Stage 3 — Learning

Audience sees:

- Lamp observing
- Lamp responding

Question:

"Can AI learn?"

Emotion:

Interest

---

# Stage 4 — Competition

Audience sees:

- Lamp outperforming
- Human frustration

Question:

"Will AI replace us?"

Emotion:

Concern

---

# Stage 5 — Collapse

Audience sees:

- Lamp failure
- Smoke
- Darkness

Question:

"Can AI exist alone?"

Emotion:

Loss

---

# Stage 6 — Compassion

Audience sees:

- Human helping AI
- Light restored

Question:

"What do they need from each other?"

Emotion:

Empathy

---

# Stage 7 — Co-Creation

Audience sees:

- Harmony
- Shared creativity

Question:

"What becomes possible together?"

Emotion:

Hope

---

# Stage 8 — WE

Audience sees:

- Final transformation
- WE symbolism
- Final musical resolution

Question answered:

"What is the future?"

Answer:

Co-creation.
