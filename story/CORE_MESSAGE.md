# Primary Message

AI is not here to replace us.

AI is here to find someone.

That someone is us.

---

# Secondary Message

The future is co-creation.

---

# What The Audience Should Feel

At the beginning:

- Curiosity
- Excitement
- Uncertainty

At the midpoint:

- Concern
- Doubt
- Tension

At the ending:

- Hope
- Understanding
- Inspiration

---

# What The Audience Should Learn

AI can:

- Learn
- Generate
- Assist
- Amplify

AI cannot replace:

- Human meaning
- Human purpose
- Human imagination

---

# What Must Never Be Communicated

Avoid suggesting:

- Humans become obsolete
- AI wins
- AI loses
- Technology is evil
- Technology is salvation

None of these are the message.

The message is collaboration.

---

# Final Realization

The audience should eventually discover:

Human + AI = WE

rather than being explicitly instructed.

The realization should feel earned.

---

# Success Test

After the performance a viewer should be able to summarize the show as:

> It wasn't about a lamp.
>
> It was about what happens when humans and AI learn to create together.
