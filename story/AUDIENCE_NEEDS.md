# Audience Needs

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

Identify the emotional and intellectual needs of the audience.

PixStars exists to address these needs.

---

# Audience Profile

Typical audience:

- Technology professionals
- Engineers
- Developers
- IT architects
- Managers
- Curious non-technical guests

---

# Current Concerns

Many audience members worry that AI will:

- Replace jobs
- Replace artists
- Replace creativity
- Replace human value

---

# Emotional Needs

The audience needs:

- Reassurance
- Perspective
- Hope
- Inspiration

---

# Intellectual Needs

The audience wants to understand:

- What AI is
- What AI is not
- Where humans fit
- How collaboration works

---

# Desired Transformation

Before:

"I am unsure what AI means for humans."

After:

"I understand that humans remain essential."

---

# Audience Promise

PixStars promises:

A thoughtful exploration of humanity and artificial intelligence through performance, storytelling, music, and interaction.

---

# Validation Question

For every scene ask:

Does this help the audience better understand their role in an AI future?

If not, reconsider the scene.
