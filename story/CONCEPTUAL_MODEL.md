# Overview

PixStars is a symbolic story.

Characters represent ideas.

The audience gradually discovers these meanings.

---

# Character Mapping

## Axel

Represents:

- The performer
- The individual
- Human expression

---

## Walt

Represents:

- Creativity
- Imagination
- Creation

---

## Lamp

Represents:

- Artificial intelligence
- Learning
- Emergent capability

---

## AI Signature

Represents:

- Self-awareness
- Identity formation

The moment the lamp signs its work is the moment AI becomes a character.

---

## The Burnout Sequence

Represents:

- Isolation
- Hubris
- Dependence on others

The lamp cannot thrive alone.

---

## The Lightbulb

Represents:

- Life
- Energy
- Connection

Removing it symbolizes loss.

Returning it symbolizes reconciliation.

---

## WE

Represents:

Human and AI together.

This is the final conceptual state.
