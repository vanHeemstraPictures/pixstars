# Layers Framework for PixStars

Version: 1.0
Status: Recommended
Project: PixStars

---

# 1. Executive Summary

PixStars is not fundamentally a technology project.

PixStars is a human story told through technology.

The purpose of this document is to ensure that every technical decision remains subordinate to the emotional and intellectual journey of the audience.

To accomplish this, PixStars adopts the Layers Framework developed by Jamie Mill.

The Layers Framework encourages creators to move from understanding people and problems toward implementation, rather than starting with technology and effects.

This aligns strongly with the core message of PixStars:

> AI is not here to replace us.
>
> AI is here to find someone.
>
> That someone is us.

# 3. Why Layers Fits PixStars

Many productions begin with:

- Lighting
- Projection
- Sound
- Technology
- Effects

PixStars intentionally reverses this.

The project begins with:

- Human fears
- Human hopes
- Human creativity
- Human identity

Technology exists only to support those themes.

---

# 4. Layer Structure

PixStars adopts the following layers.

## Layer 1 — Observed Behaviour

Understand audience beliefs and reactions.

Questions:

- What do people believe AI is?
- What are they afraid of?
- What excites them?
- What misconceptions exist?

Outputs:

- Audience observations
- Research notes
- Emotional assumptions

---

## Layer 2 — Domain

Understand the subject matter.

Topics:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Creativity
- Human expression
- Storytelling
- Art
- Co-creation

Outputs:

- Definitions
- Concepts
- Terminology

---

## Layer 3 — User Needs

Identify audience needs.

Primary audience need:

> Help me understand where humans belong in an AI future.

Outputs:

- Audience goals
- Emotional outcomes
- Learning outcomes

---

## Layer 4 — Strategy

Define the promise of the performance.

PixStars strategy:

> The future is co-creation.

AI and humans become stronger together.

Outputs:

- Core message
- Success criteria
- Experience goals

---

## Layer 5 — Conceptual Model

Create the mental model.

PixStars model:

Human → AI → Conflict → Understanding → WE

Outputs:

- Character symbolism
- Narrative architecture
- Story abstractions

---

## Layer 6 — Interaction Flow

Define audience journey.

Flow:

1. Human creates
2. AI learns
3. AI improves
4. Human doubts
5. AI fails alone
6. Human returns
7. Co-creation emerges
8. WE

Outputs:

- Story flow
- Scene sequence
- Emotional progression

---

## Layer 7 — Surface

Implementation layer.

Includes:

- Lamp movement
- Lighting
- Music
- Projection
- Lasers
- Home Assistant
- Ardour
- HiveMind
- OpenVoiceOS

Outputs:

- Technical implementation
- Cue sheets
- Hardware designs

---

# 5. Architectural Principle

No technology decision may contradict:

- Audience needs
- Story strategy
- Conceptual model

If a technology choice does not strengthen the story, it should be removed.

---

# 6. Success Definition

PixStars succeeds when audience members leave believing:

> Human creativity remains essential.

and

> The future belongs to co-creation.

Not replacement.

Not competition.

Co-creation.
