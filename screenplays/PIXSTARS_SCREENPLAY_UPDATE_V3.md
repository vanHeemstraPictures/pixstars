# PIXSTARS_SCREENPLAY_UPDATE_V3

## Digital Rain Story Arc

The digital rain is not a visual effect. It is the visual representation of A.I. learning.

### Emergence
No rain. The lamp observes.

### Discovery & First Drawing
No rain. The lamp learns.

### Recognition
The first traces of digital rain appear. Words emerge among symbols:
MICKEY, WALT, DRAW, LOOK, WHY.

### Three Deaths
The rain becomes a storm. Names, memories, sketches and fragments fall like November Rain.

### Holding
No rain. Its absence is felt.

### Return
The storm has passed. Symbols descend individually like raindrops.

### Recognition
Names appear:
AXEL, WALT, A.I.

### Final Scene
AXEL
WALT
A.I.

Behind them, the remaining rain consists primarily of W and E.

As AXEL makes room and the lamp takes the seat beside him, the audience discovers:

WE

The final W falls.
The final E falls.
The rain stops.
