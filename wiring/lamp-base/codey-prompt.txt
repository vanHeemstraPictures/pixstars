Board: ESP32-S3 N16R8 DevKitC

I need a wiring diagram only (no code). This is a "cave" enclosure under a turntable that holds all the electronics for an animatronic desk lamp. Please draw the complete wiring diagram showing all connections between these components:

ESP32-S3 N16R8 DevKitC (central controller):
- GPIO 4 -> STEP on TMC2209 stepper driver
- GPIO 5 -> DIR on TMC2209 stepper driver
- GPIO 6 -> ENABLE on TMC2209 (active low)
- GPIO 7 <- Hall effect sensor (A3144) signal (origin detect)
- GPIO 8 -> 74HCT245 pin 1 (DIR) -- half-duplex direction control (HIGH = TX, LOW = RX)
- GPIO 9 -> WS2812 rear LED ring DATA (through 330 ohm resistor, then cable column to lamp head, 16 LEDs)
- GPIO 10 -> WS2812 front LED ring DATA (through 330 ohm resistor, then cable column to lamp head, 35 LEDs)
- GPIO 15 (UART2 TX) -> 74HCT245 pin 2 (A1) -- transmit to AX-12A
- GPIO 16 (UART2 RX) <- 1K resistor <- 74HCT245 pin 3 (A2) -- receive from AX-12A (1K + 2.2K voltage divider to GND at GPIO 16, drops 4.7V to 3.23V)
- GPIO 17 (UART1 TX) -> Pololu Mini Maestro 24ch RX (serial)
- GPIO 18 (UART1 RX) <- Pololu Mini Maestro 24ch TX (serial)
- GPIO 48 -> onboard status WS2812 LED

Pololu Mini Maestro 24-channel servo controller (serial from ESP32):
- Ch0 -> MG996R servo (lower arm)
- Ch1 -> MG996R servo (elbow)
- Ch2 -> MG90S servo (neck pan, carbon fibre push-pull rod)
- Ch3-4 -> MG996R servos (spare)
- Servo power from MEAN WELL LRS-50-5 (5V)

TMC2209 stepper driver (silent StealthChop mode):
- STEP <- GPIO 4
- DIR <- GPIO 5
- EN <- GPIO 6
- Motor out -> NEMA 17 stepper (4 wires, A1/A2/B1/B2)
- VMOT -> 12V from MEAN WELL LRS-50-12
- GND -> common ground

Dynamixel AX-12A (head nod servo, in lamp head):
- TTL serial half-duplex via 74HCT245 (DIP-20) bus transceiver. DIR=GPIO 8, TX via A1 (pin 2), RX via A2 (pin 3) with 1K+2.2K voltage divider. B1 (pin 18) and B2 (pin 17) bridged together to AX-12A DATA wire.
- Power: 12V from LRS-50-12 (AX-12A accepts 9-12V)

Power supplies:
- MEAN WELL LRS-50-5 (5V, 10A): servo power, WS2812 LED rings
- MEAN WELL LRS-50-12 (12V, 4.2A): TMC2209 stepper motor, AX-12A servo, LPLDD laser driver
- All grounds tied together (common GND bus)

Cable column (routes from cave up through lamp arm to head):
- WS2812 rear ring: DATA + 5V + GND (JST-SM 3-pin)
- WS2812 front ring: DATA + 5V + GND (JST-SM 3-pin)
- AX-12A: DATA + 12V + GND
- Galvo X/Y: 4 wires (+/-5V analog from ILDA DAC DB25)
- Galvo motor power: 2 wires (+/-24V from dedicated galvo PSU)
- Laser RGB modulation: 3 wires (0-5V from LPLDD driver)

Also in cave but NOT connected to ESP32:
- ILDAWaveX16 V2 (ILDA DAC, separate ESP32-S3 board) -- receives cues from Mac Mini via WiFi, outputs ILDA DB25 to galvo driver and RGB to LPLDD driver
- 40kpps galvo driver board -- powered by +/-24V dedicated PSU
- LPLDD-1A-16V-3CH laser driver -- powered by 12V (shared LRS-50-12), 0-5V analog modulation from ILDAWaveX16 V2
